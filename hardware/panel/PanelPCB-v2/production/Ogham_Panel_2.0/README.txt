 FRONT PANEL - NO ELECTRICAL FUNCTION

  1. ALL holes, slots and internal cutouts are NON-PLATED (NPTH).
     Do not plate any hole, slot or cutout.
  2. There are no nets and no plated holes on this board.
     The PTH drill file is intentionally empty.
  3. Internal openings are defined by the Edge_Cuts layer (15 openings)
     and by the NPTH drill file (4 mounting slots, 4.5 x 3.5 mm routed).
     Route all of them; none are plated.
  4. Front copper is decorative artwork only. Where copper meets a routed
     edge, cut through it. Do not add plating, do not add copper thieving,
     and do not modify the copper or silkscreen artwork.
